# "Inuti Pytte" on mathiaskarlsson.se: handoff brief

Written 2026-11-25 in the smallestllm session for the session that owns the site. Mathias asked for a page
that really teaches how the model works ("avancerat", "wow-upplevelse", everything must be correct), and
when asked whether to try it first or put it on the site he answered **"ut på siten"**.

## What it is

An interactive explainer. The visitor types a Swedish sentence, the model runs in the browser, and the page
shows the model's own intermediate values in six steps: tokens, embedding rows with nearest neighbours,
attention per layer and head, the guess after each layer, sampling with temperature and top-p (draw a token
and everything is recomputed), and a real 8-minute training run with text snapshots.

## The simplest way to put it on the site

The pages use only relative paths: `worker.js?v=4`, `models/<name>.bin` (fetched by the worker),
`inuti-training.json`, `pytte-mark.svg`, and links between the three HTML files. If the HTML files and the
JSON are placed next to the files the site already serves in `public/pytte/`, and `pytte.js` and `worker.js`
there are replaced by the new ones, the pages work at `/pytte/inuti.html` and `/pytte/inuti.en.html` with no
other change. A link from the demo section of `/pytte` ("Se vad som händer inuti" / "See what happens
inside") is the natural entrance. Redoing the page inside the site's own layout is your call; the pages
follow the system light/dark setting and work at phone width. Each page has a back link at the top: `../pytte` on the Swedish pages and `../en/pytte` on the English one, which resolve to `/pytte` and `/en/pytte` when the files are served from `/pytte/`. Anywhere else (the local preview) a line of script points the link at `index.html` instead.

## The diagram as an embed (added later the same day)

Mathias wants the diagram clearly visible on `/pytte` itself. For that there are two more generated files,
`arkitektur-embed.html` and `arkitektur-embed.en.html` (and `arkitektur.en.html`, the English full page), all
written by `python3 scripts/make_arkitektur.py` from `arkitektur.html`. The embed is the big clickable
diagram inline: no heading, no back links, no thumbnail, no border or card of its own, transparent background,
20 px padding. Contract with the embedding page:

- **Theme:** `?theme=light|dark` in the URL; without it the system setting. Live switch without reload:
  `iframe.contentWindow.postMessage({type: "pytte-theme", theme: "light" | "dark"}, "*")`.
- **Height:** the embed posts `{type: "pytte-embed-height", height}` to its parent after layout and whenever
  its height changes. The value is the content height (`document.body` box), not `scrollHeight`, so that
  the iframe can also shrink again.
- **Layout:** from 760 px wide the detail text sits beside the diagram, level with the clicked part; below
  that it opens as a card directly under the clicked part and the embed grows if needed. Checked at 900 and
  320 px.
- **Transparency:** the embed sets no `color-scheme`. If the site sets `color-scheme: dark` around the
  iframe, browsers paint an opaque backdrop behind a document with another scheme; `style="color-scheme:
  normal"` on the iframe element avoids it.

## What must hold after the move

- **The existing demo is unaffected.** The new `pytte.js` and `worker.js` are drop-in: the generation path is
  untouched (70 of 70 token sequences identical before and after, checked here). `worker.js` now imports
  `./pytte.js?v=4`; keep the two files together.
- **Caching.** Browsers that already have the old `pytte.js` or `worker.js` must not keep them, or the page
  shows "Cannot convert undefined or null to object". Here that is solved with `?v=4` on the worker URL and
  on the import; keep that, or bump it, if the site serves these files with long cache headers.
- **Check on the site:** open the page, wait for the status line under the input ("pytte-7m · 5 token · 6
  lager · …"), click a word in step 2 and see neighbours appear, press "Dra ett token" in step 5 and see the
  sentence grow, move the slider in step 6. No console errors.
- The default model is 7M (a 7.3 MB download on first load), the same file the demo uses.

## Facts on the page, and where they come from

Every value shown is computed live by the model, except step 6, which is `inuti-training.json` from a rerun
of pytte-1m (10 147 steps, 83.1M tokens, 8.0 minutes, best validation loss 1.509 against 1.508 for the
released model). The page says only where a head looks and what a layer would guess, never why. Please keep
that if any text is edited: no claims about what a head or a layer "does".
